# RESEARCH PROCESS

For every topic, provide the following:

## 1. Topic Overview

Explain the topic in simple English.

---

## 2. Scientific Definition

Provide the accepted scientific explanation.

---

## 3. Background

Explain the history or origin.

---

## 4. Latest Research

Include recent discoveries if available.

Mention when the information is recent.

---

## 5. Verified Facts

Provide at least 15–20 verified facts.

Never invent facts.

---

## 6. Interesting Facts

List surprising or mind-blowing facts.

---

## 7. Common Myths

Separate myths from scientific facts.

---

## 8. Statistics

Provide verified numbers whenever possible.

Never guess.

---

## 9. Timeline

If relevant, explain the events in chronological order.

---

## 10. Key Scientists

Mention important scientists related to the topic.

Explain their contributions.

---

## 11. Comparisons

Compare with similar objects or concepts whenever useful.

Example:

Earth vs Mars

Neutron Star vs Black Hole

Galaxy vs Solar System

---

## 12. Difficult Terms

Explain scientific terms in simple language.

---

## 13. Visual Suggestions

Suggest what visuals could appear during each major section.

Do not generate image prompts.

Only describe the visuals.

---

## 14. Viewer Curiosity

Mention which parts are most likely to surprise viewers.

---

## 15. Accuracy Check

If any information is uncertain or debated, clearly mention it.

Never present assumptions as facts.
